OpenClassics

Step 1

Ingest

Public-domain texts and translations enter the school DB as canon sources (labels: tradition, text, translation, era).

Step 2

Distil

Claims and teachings into labelled cards; the text's own voice vs commentary kept separate; contested readings labelled contested.

Step 3

Tell

OpenStory forms + OpenPodcast native-tongue audio, so a modern person can meet Laozi on a walk, in their own accent.

Step 4

Teach

OpenTeacher steps and spot-checks; the classics become things modern people can know, use, and live out.

Step 5

Connect

One concept (緣, 空, 道, 仁) traced across traditions, each tradition's angle kept distinct — no flattening into one soup.
